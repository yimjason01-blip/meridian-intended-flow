Meridian intended flow

Get to a decision conversation, not a testing conversation.

The onboarding flow collects enough structured context, labs, imaging, and functional data before the first physician visit so the visit can focus on the few decisions that actually change trajectory.

Operating principle

Baseline is one coordinated collection event wherever possible. Genetics informs surveillance and interpretation, but it should not block the blood draw because the foundation panel already covers the key blood markers. Do not split baseline into a home capillary kit plus a separate lab visit.

Synthesis, first conversation, and cadence

Structured intake

History, goals, symptoms, family history, meds, preferences, and prior records.

  • Starts the 2 week clock
  • Creates the first problem frame

Wearable backfill

Thirty days of signals from Apple Health, Oura, Whoop, Garmin, Dexcom, or available sources.

  • Sleep, activity, HRV, glucose
  • Interpretation context for labs

Adaptive lab plan

Universal foundation panel plus intake-triggered add-ons. The panel is composed before collection, but not blocked by genetics.

  • Cardiometabolic, renal, inflammatory
  • Hormones, thyroid, PSA when appropriate
  • Quest venous rail for full baseline

Genetics kit

Runs in parallel. It informs surveillance, imaging, and interpretation more than lab composition.

Non-blocking

Mobile phlebotomy

One coordinated home or office blood draw. Quest remains the venous processing rail.

  • Baseline quality over novelty
  • Full CBC and chemistry capability
  • Capillary kits move to rechecks

Imaging rail

Parallel scheduling for tests that change the first conversation.

  • DEXA for body composition
  • CAC for midlife CVD framing
  • Cancer or MRI path when risk-gated

Functional rail

Measures capacity and resilience rather than only disease risk.

  • CPET or VO2 evidence
  • Grip strength
  • Force plate or movement screen

Result intake

Labs, images, genetics, wearable history, and functional data land into one physician pre-read packet.

  • No raw-data dump
  • Domain summaries with deltas

Doctor pre-read

Delivered 24 to 48 hours before the first visit. Written like a physician note, not a portal notification.

  • Top findings
  • Open decisions
  • Where uncertainty remains

First conversation

The physician visit is decision-only: what to do now, what to watch, and what not to over-medicalize.

  • Top 3 to 5 actions
  • Domain risk tradeoffs
  • Patient-specific priorities

Care plan release

Actions become a plan with accountability, timing, orders, and a monitoring schema.

  • Risk reduction bucket
  • Monitoring bucket
  • Clear ownership

Cadence moat

Follow-up is risk-tiered by domain. Tasso-style capillary kits belong here for focused rechecks, not in baseline.

  • Labs by domain cadence
  • Wearables as continuous context
  • Imaging and function at slower intervals
