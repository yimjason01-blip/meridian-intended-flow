DECISION POINTS (the only branches that matter)

Risk tier? at D+1 — sets phlebotomy add-on panels and gates imaging.
Imaging gated? at D+3 — risk ≥ medium triggers imaging order.
Cadence tier? at the visit — sets the recheck interval (30/90/180).

THE VISIT IS THE PIVOT

Everything before D+14 builds the dossier. The visit is where the physician decides interventions, risk tier, and cadence. After the visit, the workflow becomes a recurring loop indexed by the cadence tier — not a new linear flow.

THE LOOP IS THE PRODUCT

Cadence is not a final state; it is the steady-state operating mode. Re-synthesis at +180d feeds the next cadence schedule. Tier can move (low → med, med → high) as trajectory data accumulates. This is the recurring revenue surface.
